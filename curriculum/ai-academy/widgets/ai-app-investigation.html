AI Academy - Module 1 project

AI App Investigation

You have about 35 minutes. Research and test one AI app, then share what you found. Check off each step as you go.

Stay safe: never type personal information into an AI tool.

  • No full name, address, phone number, school name, passwords or photos of yourself or friends.
  • Use only the apps your teacher says are OK.

1. Pick your app

2. Test three kinds of prompts

Pick one prompt from each group. For each test, write the prompt and a 1-2 sentence summary of the answer.

Fact-checking: is the information accurate?

Tip: check the answer against your textbook or another trusted source.

Creative writing: can it write something new and clear?

Tip: for the rewrite, paste a paragraph you wrote about a school topic - nothing personal.

Capabilities and limits: what can and can't it do?

Tip: does the app admit what it does not know, or does it guess?

3. Judge what you saw

4. Turn in and share

All done, great work!
