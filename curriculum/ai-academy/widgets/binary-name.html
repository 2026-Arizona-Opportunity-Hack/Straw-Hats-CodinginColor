Binary Letters

A bit is one tiny switch: off is 0, on is 1.

Eight bits make a byte. Each switch is worth double the one to its right.

Add up the switches that are on to get a number. Computers save letters as numbers, so 65 means A.

255 is the biggest number one byte can hold. Every switch is on.

Challenge: your first initial

  1. Find the capital letter of your first initial in the table below. Keep it in your head - you do not need to type it anywhere.
  2. Flip switches until the number matches. Start with the biggest switch that fits, then add smaller ones.
  3. Check that the "Letter" box shows your initial. Write your 8 bits (like 01000001) on paper.

Hint: how to make A (65)

65 = 64 + 1. Turn on the 64 switch and the 1 switch. Everything else stays off, so A is 01000001.
